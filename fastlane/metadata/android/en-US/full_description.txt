Mozhi is a fast, offline English to Malayalam dictionary for Android.

Type a word and get instant suggestions as you type, with the matching part highlighted. Tap any suggestion to see its Malayalam meanings, numbered and easy to read.

Everything works fully offline: the complete dictionary of 59,040 English words and over 125,000 Malayalam definitions ships inside the app. No account, no network, no tracking, no ads.

- Instant prefix search with highlighted matches
- Numbered Malayalam meanings for every word
- System, light, and dark (AMOLED black) themes with Material You dynamic colors
- Clean Malayalam-first interface set in the Manjari typeface

Data adapted from the Mozhi open dictionary project.
